📦 Orders & Tracking

How do I track my order?
Go to My Orders from your account menu, choose the order, and tap Track Order. You'll see live status updates: Placed → Packed → Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered.
Can I cancel my order?
Yes — as long as the order hasn't shipped yet. Open My Orders, select the order and tap Cancel Order. Once it's shipped, cancellation isn't possible, but you can request a return after delivery.
My order is delayed. What do I do?
Delays sometimes happen due to weather or courier volume. Check the tracking page first — if there's no update for over 2 days, chat with our support assistant (bottom-right corner) with your order number and we'll look into it right away.

💳 Payments

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept major debit/credit cards, UPI, net banking, and Cash on Delivery (where available). You'll see all valid options at checkout.
My payment failed but money was deducted — what now?
Don't worry, this is usually a temporary bank-side hold. It's auto-reversed within 5-7 business days. If it isn't, chat with our support assistant with your payment reference and we'll help you sort it out.

↩️ Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?
Most items can be returned within a set number of days of delivery, as long as they're unused and in original packaging. Check the specific policy on the product page, or ask our support assistant.
How do I start a return?
  1. Go to My Orders and open the delivered order
  2. Tap Return Item and choose a reason
  3. Schedule a pickup or drop-off, as shown
  4. Refund is issued once the item passes a quality check
When will I get my refund?
Refunds are usually processed within a few business days after the returned item is received and checked, and then reflect in your original payment method per your bank's timeline.

🛍️ Shopping

How do I know if an item is in stock?
The product page shows real-time stock status. If it's sold out, you can still ask our support assistant to check availability or restock timing.
Can I change my cart or address before checkout?
Absolutely — open your cart anytime before payment to update quantities, remove items, or change the delivery address.
