✦ How It Works

Start Selling in 6 Simple Steps

From your first request to a live shop, our team guides you at every stage.

1

Submit a Request

Fill in the short vendor form with your business details. You get a request code instantly to track your status.

2

We Contact You

Our team reviews your request and reaches out to you to understand your business and guide you further.

3

Approved & Account Created

Once your request is approved, our admin creates your vendor account. Your username and temporary password are sent to your registered email and mobile number.

4

Login & Change Password

Log in with your credentials. On your first login you will be asked to set a new, secure password of your own.

5

Complete Onboarding

Complete your onboarding, then use your vendor dashboard to set up your store and add your products.

6

Shop Approved & Live

Our admin reviews your shop. Once approved, your shop goes live and customers can start ordering.
